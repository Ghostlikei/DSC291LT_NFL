Wolpert–Macready — Proof: Inside/Outside Split

The Key Structural Insight

Write $S_{\text{in}} = \{x_1,\ldots,x_m\}$ (queried), $\;S_{\text{out}} = \mathcal{X}\setminus S_{\text{in}}$ (remaining $|\mathcal{X}|-m$ points). Specifying $f$ = specifying $(f|_{S_{\text{in}}}, f|_{S_{\text{out}}})$ independently, so $\displaystyle\sum_f = \sum_{f|_{S_{\text{in}}}}\sum_{f|_{S_{\text{out}}}}$.

Chain-rule decomposition:

$$P(\vec y^{(m+1)} \mid f, m+1, \mathcal{A}) = \underbrace{P(y_{m+1} \mid \vec y^{(m)}, f, \mathcal{A})}_{\mathbf{1}[f(x_{m+1})=y_{m+1}]\;\text{depends on }f|_{S_{\text{out}}}} \cdot \underbrace{P(\vec y^{(m)} \mid f, m, \mathcal{A})}_{\text{depends on }f|_{S_{\text{in}}}}$$

Since $x_{m+1} = \mathcal{A}(D_m) \notin S_{\text{in}}$ (no-revisit), the two factors act on disjoint coordinates of $f$:

$$\sum_f P(\vec y^{(m+1)} \mid f, m+1, \mathcal{A}) \;=\; \underbrace{\Bigl[\sum_{f|_{S_{\text{out}}}} \mathbf{1}[f(x_{m+1})=y_{m+1}]\Bigr]}_{\text{outside sum}} \;\cdot\; \underbrace{\Bigl[\sum_{f|_{S_{\text{in}}}} P(\vec y^{(m)} \mid f, m, \mathcal{A})\Bigr]}_{\text{inside sum}}$$
Core observation. Every algorithm merely picks coordinates of $f$ in some order. Summing over all $f$ makes every ordering — and therefore every algorithm — equivalent.
